## What you are looking at

This demo asks one question: can Stopcock turn a frequency balance target into a
synth patch, then prove the patch really makes that sound?

The bars are an EQ-style target. Taller means louder in that frequency band:
bass on the left, mids in the middle, brightness and air on the right. The chart
compares the same 16 bands three ways: grey target, blue model prediction, and
purple measured audio from the browser analyser.

## Why it is interesting

It is a closed loop. Autodiff moves the patch knobs, synth turns those knobs into
sound, signal measures the sound, and SVG shows the disagreement. When the model
and the measured audio diverge, you can see the miss instead of hiding it.

## What each library is doing

[autodiff](/libraries/autodiff/) moves the patch. [signal](/libraries/signal/)
verifies the sound. [synth](/libraries/synth/) makes the audio. [svg](/libraries/svg/)
draws the spectrum, fingerprint, landscape, spectrogram, and poster. [color](/libraries/color/)
keeps the palette readable, including the colour-vision lenses.
